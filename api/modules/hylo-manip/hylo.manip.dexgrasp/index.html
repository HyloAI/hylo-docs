    hylo.manip.dexgrasp¶

    Package: hylo-manip · Core contracts

    dexgrasp ¶

    Multi-finger grasp planning for any dexterous hand (a HandModel: HandSpec plus its MJCF) on an object given as points or a mesh.

    Object: one or more convex parts (the hull of the points, of the mesh, or of each given part), so the signed distance to the object is the smallest of the parts' hull distances and a ray's entry into the object is exact. A partial point cloud seen from above is extruded down to the table first (the hidden underside is solid).

    Planning, per candidate hand pose: 1. sample: approach directions (+x of the TCP) over the sphere, minus those from below the table, times rolls about the approach (the +y closing axis); the TCP aims at the object's centre (plus offsets during refinement); 2. approach: the open hand (a rigid point set) slides along the approach until its first point would enter the object or the table (ray-hull entry distances, exact), never past the centre; 3. close: each finger moves from the open command towards the closing command (HandSpec.closed for "power", the "pinch" preset for "pinch") until its links touch the object (a sweep, then bisection per finger); 4. score: contacts are the finger and palm samples within a few millimetres of the surface (one per link, the hull facet's normal); the epsilon measure of their friction-cone wrenches (force closure when positive), the total normal force needed to hold the object against gravity, penetration, how many fingers touch, and an optional preferred approach direction or pose; 5. refine: the best few are perturbed (offset, tilt, roll) for a couple of rounds and re-scored (a short cross-entropy search over the pose; the fingers re-close each time).

    plan() returns DexGrasp records, best first; to_goals() turns them into toolkit Goals (pre-pose back along the approach with the hand open, then the hand closing to a squeeze command past the contact angles).

    evaluate ¶
