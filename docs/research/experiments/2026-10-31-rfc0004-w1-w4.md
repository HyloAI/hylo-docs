# RFC-0004 W1-W4: external data, world model, noise-space steering, shadow mode (2026-10-31)

## W1. External data

- Ingest: the local LeRobotDataset v3 exported by the self-training smoke test (5 episodes, 1116 frames) became the
  run `external:selftrain-smoke-demos`. States, actions, both cameras (every 10th frame plus the last) and the
  dataset's own `next.success` labels were kept. Took 8 s.
- Contamination check, validated on LIBERO-10 gate runs: the same init state across two runs is at distance 0.0;
  different init states of the same task are at least 0.0027 apart (median 0.014). The tolerance is 0.001. The
  ingested demos (init state 0) did not match any of 800 protected episodes.
- Video path, end to end:
  1. An image-only judge (agentview thumbnails, no proprio), trained on the search split: AUC 0.94 on 600
     search_val episodes; 44% recall at 95% precision (`ready`).
  2. 400 validation-split episodes (never used for training or calibration) were written out as plain image
     folders, with their outcomes removed.
  3. Ingested with `video.frames` and labelled with `hylo label`: 83 labelled successes, of which 81 were real
     (precision 0.976, recall 0.37). The other 317 episodes stay unlabeled, and none were called failures.

## W2. World model (state)

`world_model.state` was trained on runs of the test double (testing.point_reach, then called the mock environment) (search split; P-controllers with kp 0.3, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0;
47.5k transitions). Success on gate init states, real environment vs inside the model:

| kp | 0.1 | 0.3 | 0.5 | 1.0 | 2.0 | 4.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| real | 0.03 | 0.05 | 0.07 | 0.73 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| model | 0.03 | 0.03 | 0.08 | 0.90 | 1.00 | 1.00 |

The ranking is right, including the gains 0.1 and 4.0, which never appeared in training. The model is optimistic
near the threshold (kp 1.0). In a unit test, the prescreen rejects kp 0.1 against a kp 1.0 parent before any
real rollout. This is a state model: it cannot host a VLA that acts on images.

## W3. Noise-space steering (offline counterpart of DSRL)

Mechanism, on real SmolVLA: replaying a recorded chunk's seed reproduces the chunk exactly (max diff 0.0); the
regenerated noise at weight 1 reproduces it under another seed (0.0); weight 0 is the plain policy (0.0).

- Bank: 522 noise segments from 101 successful search-split episodes (init states 0-19).
- Evaluation: search_val (init states 20-29, unseen by the bank), paired with common random numbers against the
  same agent without the bank (success 0.54).
- The prior was used at about 42% of decisions (similarity floor 0.95).

| weight | success | wins / losses vs control | p (one-sided) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3 | 0.55 | 6 / 5 | 0.50 |
| 0.6 | 0.54 | 9 / 9 | 0.59 |

Null result. Noise steering does not hurt, unlike the action prior (-8 points on unseen states), presumably
because the chunk is still produced by the velocity field for the current observation. But the noise of past
successes carries no transferable information about which mode succeeds at a new state. This matches the earlier
diagnosis for action memory: retrieval at unseen states returns "what the policy usually does", not "what works".
Learning a state-conditioned noise policy from outcomes (actual DSRL) needs online RL with many rollouts. Not
proposed as a candidate.

## W4. Shadow mode

On the test-double campaign, a candidate shadowing the active version over 6628 decisions:
- outcomes identical to running the active agent alone (success 1.0);
- the candidate's executed prefix would have differed by 0.146 on average (p95 max 0.43);
- no shadow errors.

In tests, background mode gives the same outcomes and one comparison per decision.
