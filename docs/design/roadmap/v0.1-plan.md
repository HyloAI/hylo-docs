## Progress (2026-10-30)

Done ahead of plan: LIBERO direct adapter (`adapters/hylo-env-libero`, out of process, CPU rendering), AgentServer +
`adapters/hylo-policy-lerobot` (SmolVLA on CPU), parallel evaluation workers, SafetyShield clip tolerance, trace image
stride. SmolVLA (`lerobot/smolvla_libero`) on LIBERO with 5 init states per task: spatial 0.70, object 0.66,
goal 0.78, libero_10 0.42 (see `examples/configs/libero_smolvla.yaml`). Validated against LeRobot's own `lerobot-eval` on libero_spatial: 0.64 vs our 0.70 on the same 50 episodes (not significant, same per-task pattern). Still open from weeks 1-4: CI, Approver-chain port, WebSocket
transport, openpi client.
